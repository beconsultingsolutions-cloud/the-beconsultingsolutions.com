Brandon Ellis Bynum is the Founder and CEO of The B.E. Consulting Solutions (BECS), a business systems and fractional COO firm he started in 2022. He is the creator of P.E.S., the Plan. Evolve. Succeed. framework that shapes every BECS engagement.

He builds and runs what he recommends.

Brandon doesn't just advise on systems. Each of these ventures is proof of the method, built in the open.

He serves as COO of an apparel agency, where he architected its client portal and sales pipeline. He built a leather accessories brand from concept to launch-ready. And he served as fractional COO for a specialty retail brand, delivering its full operating system and AI layer.

Brandon is also the author of ME & THEM, a book and philosophy platform connected to the 4FREQ lifestyle framework. Through BE University, he teaches founders the power of branding, digital systems, and vibe coding, so they can build what they used to have to hire out.

He works with founder-led brands: coaches and consultants, wellness and beauty businesses, and creative and product founders. Based in Northern Illinois, he works with clients across the US, virtually and in person.
